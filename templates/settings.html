Inventory Threshold Rules

Automated alert logic

Low Stock Condition: When on-hand inventory drops to or below a product's configured reorder level (quantity ≤ reorder_level), Stockly automatically surfaces the item in the Dashboard Alerts panel and Reports view.

Stock Depletion (Out of Stock): Reaching exactly 0 units triggers an urgent Out of Stock condition, preventing outgoing transactions until new inventory is received via Stock In.

Audit Integrity: Stock movements are stored in append-only transaction logs. Archiving an item preserves all historical transactions without deletion.
