Operational Threshold Rules

Automated inventory logic

Low Stock Condition: When on-hand inventory drops to or below a product's configured reorder level (quantity ≤ reorder_level), Stockly automatically flags the item in the Dashboard Alerts panel and Reports view.

Depletion Prevention: Reaching exactly 0 units triggers an urgent Out of Stock status. The Stock Out console automatically blocks fulfillment requests that exceed available inventory.

Audit Integrity: Stock movements are stored in append-only transaction logs. Product archiving performs a soft-delete, ensuring audit trails remain completely immutable.
